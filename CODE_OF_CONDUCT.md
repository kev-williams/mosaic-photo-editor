# Code of conduct

Mosaic Photo-Editor is a friendly, welcoming project. In issues, pull requests and discussions:

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent; many people here are not developers.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person.
- No harassment, insults or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Respect privacy: never post photos of people without their permission, and never share someone's
  personal information.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments and block participants who don't follow these guidelines.
To report a problem, contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile.

This code of conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
